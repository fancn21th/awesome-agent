# AI Usage Tips

This checklist is useful when you want to learn a new topic, prepare for exams, fill knowledge gaps quickly, or turn scattered information into an actionable study plan.

## 1. Ask AI to build a learning ladder first

Do not jump straight into details. First, ask AI to break a topic into levels from beginner to advanced. That makes it easier to see where you are and what order to study in.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
I want to learn [topic]. Please break it down into 4 to 6 levels from beginner to advanced.
For each level, explain:
1. The core concepts I need to master
2. Common mistakes or misunderstandings
3. What I should be able to do before moving to the next level
4. Recommended practice methods
```

## 2. Ask AI to identify the most important 20%

Most subjects have a critical 20% that drives most of the results. Finding that first is usually much more efficient, especially for exams, interviews, or short-term intensive study.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
I plan to spend 20 hours learning [topic]. Please identify the most important 20% of the material.
Then turn it into a 10-session study plan, with each session lasting 2 hours.
Each session should include:
1. Learning objective
2. Key concepts
3. Practice task
4. Recommended resources
5. Review questions
```

## 3. Let AI quiz you until you truly know it

Many people think they understand something when they have only seen it once. If AI asks one question at a time and keeps probing, it becomes much easier to expose weak spots.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
Act as my examiner and quiz me on [topic].
Ask only one question at a time. After I answer:
1. Tell me whether I got it right
2. Point out missing parts and mistakes
3. Show me a stronger sample answer
4. Continue with the next question

If I keep getting things wrong, lower the difficulty, fill in the basics first, and then continue the quiz.
```

## 4. Ask AI to generate a cheat sheet

After one round of study, the most useful thing is often not rereading long material, but compressing it into a quick-reference sheet you can review fast.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
Please turn [topic] into a one-page cheat sheet that is concise and easy to review.
Include:
1. Core concepts
2. Common formulas or rules
3. Common points of confusion
4. High-frequency mistakes
5. One minimal example
```

## 5. Ask AI to filter resources instead of collecting everything

Most people do not have too few resources. They have too many. Ask AI to filter resources based on your goal, time, and current level before you start.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
I want to learn [topic]. My current level is [current level], my goal is [goal], and I can spend [time].
Please recommend only 5 resources that fit me best, and explain why each one is a good fit.

Also label each resource with:
1. Beginner or advanced
2. Expected learning cost
3. Whether it is suitable for fast-track learning
4. The order in which I should use it
```

## 6. Use the Feynman technique and let AI find the gaps

Real understanding shows up when you can explain something clearly. Your job is to explain it. AI's job is to point out what is inaccurate, incomplete, or still unclear.

Prompt you can copy:

```text
I will explain [topic] in my own words. Do not rush to give me the answer.
First, evaluate my explanation and tell me:
1. What is inaccurate
2. What is incomplete
3. Which terms I may have memorized without truly understanding
4. What I still could not explain clearly to a beginner

Then give me a clearer and easier-to-understand version based on my weak points.
```

## Suggested workflow

If you want to use AI more effectively for learning, follow this order:

1. Build the learning ladder.
2. Identify the critical 20%.
3. Study and practice.
4. Let AI keep testing you.
5. Generate a cheat sheet and use the Feynman technique to find gaps.

The goal is not to ask more questions. The goal is to let AI participate in the full learning loop: planning, studying, testing, reviewing, and reinforcing.
